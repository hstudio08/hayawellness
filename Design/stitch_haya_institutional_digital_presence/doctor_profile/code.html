Professional Biography

Dr. Elena Rostova brings over 15 years of exceptional clinical experience in cardiovascular medicine, merging rigorous academic training with a compassionate, patient-centered approach. Her methodology focuses on comprehensive wellness, viewing the heart as the center of holistic health rather than merely a mechanical pump.

Before joining Haya Wellness, Dr. Rostova spearheaded the Structural Heart Disease program at the prestigious Emerald District Memorial, where she introduced pioneering catheter-based valve repair techniques. Her ongoing research focuses on the intersection of lifestyle modifications and genetic predispositions in heart disease.
